This page is for **advanced operators and developers**.

## Overview

CSM’s update tooling is designed to be conservative and avoid unsafe restarts during matches.

## What CSM watches for

When used with MatchZy Enhanced, the server logs may include markers like:

```text
[MATCHZY_UPDATE_AVAILABLE] required_version=<number>
[MATCHZY_UPDATE_SHUTDOWN] required_version=<number>
```

CSM can also detect legacy shutdown markers depending on your setup.
