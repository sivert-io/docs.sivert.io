---
title: Shuffle tournament summary
description: How shuffle tournaments work and when to use them.
---

Shuffle tournaments are a tournament type where players compete **individually**, and teams are **re-balanced automatically** between rounds based on rating.

## When to use shuffle

- You don’t have fixed teams (mixes, PUGs, community events).
- You want “fairer” matches round-to-round as ratings update.

## What to expect

- **Players, not teams, are the primary unit**: standings and ratings are per-player.
- **Teams are re-generated** between rounds based on current ratings.
- **New players start at a neutral rating** (and stabilize after a few games).

## Operator tips

- Keep the player pool size consistent (late joins/leaves can make balancing harder).
- Use a short match format (BO1) for smoother rotations during events.
